Our Story: Dagii Scrubs

Founded right here in **Meru Town**, Dagii Scrubs was established with a clear mission: to provide the hardworking medical professionals of Kenya with uniforms that don't compromise on **comfort, durability, or style**.

We understand the demanding environments healthcare workers operate in. Your scrubs are more than just a uniform; they're a daily tool. That's why we meticulously select our fabrics for maximum performance—easy to clean, quick to dry, and engineered to last.

From a local Meru startup to a trusted supplier, our commitment remains the same: supporting those who care for our community.

Our Mission

To empower healthcare professionals by providing high-quality, innovative scrubs that blend peak functionality with modern, professional aesthetics.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and recognized brand for medical apparel in Kenya, synonymous with reliability and unmatched customer care.
